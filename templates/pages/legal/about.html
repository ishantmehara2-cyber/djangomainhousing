About The Home First

Your Property. Your Possibilities.

The Home First is a real estate platform operated by AINexTech LLP.

Website: www.thehomefirst.com | Email: connectthehomefirst@gmail.com | Phone: +91-77382 30656

Welcome to The Home First, a digital platform designed to make your property journey simpler, smarter and more convenient.

Finding the right property, connecting with the right people, exploring home loan options and arranging rental documentation can often involve multiple platforms and complicated processes.

At The Home First, our aim is to bring these property-related services together in one convenient destination.

Whether you are looking to buy your dream home, find a rental property, explore commercial spaces, showcase property listings or enquire about home loan assistance, we aim to make your journey easier.

We believe that every property decision begins with the right information, meaningful connections and a platform that helps people take their next step with confidence.

Our Vision

To build a convenient and accessible digital destination for property discovery and related real estate services, connecting people with opportunities and helping simplify property-related decisions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make property discovery and related services easier by:

  • Bringing property buyers, sellers, tenants, owners and Real Estate Partners together.
  • Making residential and commercial property discovery convenient.
  • Providing access to home loan enquiry facilities and banking partner information.
  • Offering rental agreement-related service enquiries.
  • Helping eligible Real Estate Partners showcase their property listings.
  • Creating a user-friendly and transparent digital experience.
  • Continuously improving the way people discover and enquire about properties.

What We Offer

1. Buy Properties

Looking for a property to call your own? Explore available residential and commercial property listings, review the information provided and connect with the relevant property owner or representative.

2. Rent Properties

Discover properties available for rent according to your requirements. Explore available listings, review property details and submit an enquiry to connect with the relevant representative.

3. Sell or List Properties

Property owners and authorised representatives can explore opportunities to showcase their properties through The Home First. Eligible Real Estate Partners can register on the platform and, after completing the required verification and approval process, access property listing facilities.

4. Commercial Properties

Explore commercial property opportunities, including spaces suitable for offices, shops and other business requirements, subject to the listings available on the platform.

5. Home Loan Assistance

Planning to purchase a property and exploring financing options? The Home First provides access to banking partner information and a facility to submit home loan enquiries.

Customers can share their basic requirements through the home loan enquiry form. The relevant team or financial institution may contact them regarding the enquiry.

Loan eligibility, interest rates, processing charges, approval and disbursement are determined by the concerned financial institution. The Home First is not itself a bank or lending institution.

6. Rental Agreement Services

A rental agreement is an important part of the rental process. The Home First provides access to rental agreement-related services so customers can submit their requirements and enquire about the assistance available.

Applicable charges, document requirements, service scope and timelines will be communicated before a paid service is confirmed.

7. Real Estate Partner Network

The Home First provides an opportunity for eligible real estate professionals and authorised property representatives to register and showcase property listings.

Registration may require government or business registration documents, such as:

  • Gumasta / Shop and Establishment registration
  • MSME registration
  • Udyam registration
  • GST registration

Submitted documents are subject to review. Registration does not automatically guarantee approval or permission to publish properties.
