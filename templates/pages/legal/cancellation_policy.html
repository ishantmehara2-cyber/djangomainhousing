Cancellation Policy

The Home First ("THF") | Operated by AINexTech LLP | Contact: connectthehomefirst@gmail.com | Phone: +91-77382 30656

1. Introduction

This Cancellation Policy explains how users may request cancellation of services ordered through The Home First, a real estate platform operated by AINexTech LLP.

Cancellation conditions depend on the type of service and the stage of processing.

2. Free Property and Home Loan Enquiries

Users may request that an enquiry submitted through the website be withdrawn or closed.

However, withdrawing an enquiry does not necessarily reverse communication that has already been sent to a property representative or financial institution.

Where possible, The Home First will stop further processing of the enquiry after receiving a valid request.

3. Rental Agreement Services

Customers wishing to cancel a rental agreement-related service should contact us as soon as possible.

Cancellation eligibility may depend on:

  • Whether the request has been accepted.
  • Whether document preparation has started.
  • Whether the service has been completed.
  • Whether a third-party service provider has already been engaged.
  • The terms communicated at the time of payment.

Any applicable cancellation charges must be disclosed before the customer confirms the paid service.

4. Paid Listing or Promotional Services

If paid listing or promotional services are introduced, cancellation conditions will be communicated separately before purchase.

The cancellation outcome may depend on whether the service has been activated or promotional work has already been performed.

5. How to Request Cancellation

Send an email to:

connectthehomefirst@gmail.com

Subject line: Cancellation Request – The Home First

Please include:

  • Full name.
  • Registered mobile number.
  • Registered email address.
  • Order or service reference number.
  • Date of purchase or request.
  • Reason for cancellation.

6. Cancellation Confirmation

Our team will review the request and communicate whether cancellation has been accepted. If a refund is applicable, it will be handled according to the Refund Policy and applicable law.

7. Cancellation by The Home First

The Home First may cancel or discontinue a service where the service cannot be provided, required information is not supplied, fraudulent or misleading information is identified, or a technical/operational issue prevents fulfilment.

Where payment has been collected, any applicable refund will be handled according to the Refund Policy and applicable law.
