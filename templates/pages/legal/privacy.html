Privacy Policy

The Home First ("THF", "we", "our" or "us") | Website: https://www.thehomefirst.com/

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Home First ("THF", "we", "our" or "us"), a real estate platform operated by AINexTech LLP.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and share information when you visit or use our website, https://www.thehomefirst.com/, create an account, submit an enquiry, register as a Real Estate Partner or use any of our services.

By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect the following categories of information.

A. Personal Information

  • Full name.
  • Mobile number.
  • Email address.
  • Residential or business address, where required.
  • City, state, locality and PIN code.
  • Other information voluntarily provided through website forms.

B. Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • Registered email address or username.
  • Securely stored authentication credentials.
  • Account type.
  • Account status.
  • Relevant account settings and activity.

Passwords should be stored using secure password hashing and must not be stored in readable plain text.

C. Real Estate Partner Information

Individuals registering as Real Estate Partners may be asked to provide:

  • Full name.
  • Mobile number.
  • Email address.
  • Business or agency name.
  • Business address.
  • City, state, locality and PIN code.
  • Government or business registration documents (Gumasta / Shop and Establishment registration, MSME, Udyam or GST documentation).
  • Additional information reasonably required for verification.

D. Property Information

When a property is submitted for listing, we may collect:

  • Property title and description.
  • Property category and transaction type.
  • Property location.
  • Price or rental amount.
  • Property specifications, amenities and facilities.
  • Property photographs and uploaded media.
  • Ownership or authorisation-related information, where requested.
  • Contact information of the submitting user or representative.

E. Enquiry Information

We collect information relevant to the enquiry you submit:

  • Home Loan Enquiries: Full name, mobile number, email address, employment type, net monthly income, desired loan amount, preferred bank.
  • Rental Agreement Enquiries: Name and contact details, property/rental details, required service details, submitted form details.
  • Government Document Assistance Requests: Full name, mobile number, email address, business/agency name, city, assistance requirements.
  • Property Enquiries: Contact information, property reference/listing info, enquiry message, voluntary details.

F. Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, pages visited, date and time of access, website interaction data, and cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Creating and managing user accounts.
  2. Reviewing Real Estate Partner registrations.
  3. Verifying documents submitted during registration.
  4. Receiving and managing property listings.
  5. Responding to property enquiries.
  6. Processing home loan enquiries.
  7. Responding to rental agreement service requests.
  8. Handling government or business document assistance requests.
  9. Sending email verification codes.
  10. Sending enquiry confirmations and service-related updates.
  11. Communicating with users regarding their requests.
  12. Maintaining website security.
  13. Preventing fraudulent activity and misuse.
  14. Improving website functionality and user experience.
  15. Maintaining business records.
  16. Meeting applicable legal obligations.

4. Email Verification and Notifications

Certain forms and account processes may require email verification.

  • A one-time verification code may be sent to the email address provided.
  • The code is intended to verify access to that email address.
  • The code may have a limited validity period.
  • Verification information is used for the relevant verification process.

We may also send enquiry confirmations, account notifications and service-related communications.

Users should never share passwords or verification codes with anyone claiming to represent The Home First through an ordinary email or message.

5. How We Share Information

We do not intend to sell personal information as a standalone product. Information may be shared in limited circumstances.

A. Property Owners and Representatives

When you submit an enquiry about a property, relevant contact information and enquiry details may be shared with the concerned property owner, Real Estate Partner or authorised representative so that they can respond.

B. Banks and Financial Institutions

When you submit a home loan enquiry, relevant information may be shared with the concerned financial institution or authorised representative where necessary to handle your request and where permitted by applicable law.

C. Service Providers

We may use third-party providers for website hosting, email delivery, maintenance, security, analytics, technical support, and payment processing (if applicable).

D. Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, a valid legal process, a court order or a lawful request from an authorised government authority.

E. Business Transfers

If the business undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring or transfer, relevant information may be transferred subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain user sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, understand usage, and support website security and performance.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse, including access restrictions, authentication controls, secure password handling, secure data transmission, file upload validation, and security monitoring.

However, no internet-based platform or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for account administration, enquiry management, service delivery, verification, record keeping, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent. Contact us regarding privacy-related requests.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including banking partners and service providers. We do not control their privacy practices. Please review third-party privacy policies before submitting personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed towards children. Where applicable law requires parental consent or additional safeguards, we will follow applicable requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
